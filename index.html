The Diagnosis: Energy, EROI, and Overshoot

The modern economy treats energy as a simple line item, blinding us to the fact that fossil hydrocarbons are the literal engine of civilization. We have burned through millions of years of ancient sunlight in a 250-year "Carbon Pulse." As the Energy Return on Investment (EROI) plummets, the massive, complex systems of the global economy are beginning to stutter under the thermodynamic weight of ecological overshoot.

The Thermodynamic Squeeze

The Jevons Paradox Technological efficiency does not reduce resource use on a macroeconomic scale; it accelerates it. As we make systems more efficient, the Economic Superorganism reinvests those gains to extract more materials, compounding overshoot rather than solving it.
The EROI Cliff Early in the 20th century, discovering oil required minimal energy (EROI > 100:1). Today, shale oil, deep-water drilling, and complex renewables offer drastically lower returns (< 15:1). It takes increasing amounts of energy simply to acquire energy, starving the broader economy of surplus.

The Human Trap: The Dopamine Superhighway

We do not just face an environmental crisis; we face a mismatch between our ancestral brains and a modern, high-throughput environment. The Economic Superorganism hijacks our evolutionary drives for status and novelty, trapping us on a "Dopamine Superhighway." This overstimulation creates a profound meaning crisis, making the slow, intergenerational work of bioregional resilience difficult to focus on.

The Bioregional Blueprint

We cannot wait for top-down technological salvation to protect us from civilizational descent. The response to the Metacrisis must be scaled to the watershed and the community. By applying the "Head, Place, Heart, Hands" framework, we can build localized, regenerative lifeboats capable of weathering the Great Simplification.

Unbolting from the Titanic requires cognitive clarity. We must adopt "Wide-Boundary Thinking" to see how energy, the economy, and the biosphere interrelate.

  • Recognize the Sunk Cost of the "Endless Summer" progress narrative.
  • Accept that "Away" does not exist; waste cycles back into the biosphere.
  • Understand that the Superorganism prioritizes power over life.

Redefining our address from political zip codes to ecological realities. Reinhabitation is the practice of becoming truly native to your bioregion.

  • Map your local watershed and the geological chassis beneath your feet.
  • Conduct a Flora & Fauna census to know your non-human neighbors.
  • Embrace Traditional Ecological Knowledge (TEK) to understand place-based stewardship.

Navigating the psychological toll of descent. We must metabolize "Solastalgia" (the grief of losing a home while still in it) to avoid collapse paralysis.

  • Practice Active Hope: not a feeling, but a daily, deliberate muscle.
  • Cultivate Revolutionary Love to metabolize social friction and polarization.
  • Adopt a "Post-Tragic" orientation: accepting limits while acting with joyful agency.

The gritty, physical work of the descent. Transitioning from isolated consumers to interdependent community mechanics.

  • Rebuild the Soil Sponge: Slow, spread, and sink water to buffer climate whiplash.
  • Create the Commons: Establish tool libraries, crop swaps, and local credit systems.
  • Commit to the 9-by-21 Challenge: tangible bioregional actions executed locally.
